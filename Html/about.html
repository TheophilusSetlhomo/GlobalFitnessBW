About Global Fitness Botswana

Global Fitness Botswana is a fitness and wellness company created to help people begin and continue their fitness journey in a comfortable, safe, and supportive environment. The company was established in 2010 and has grown into a trusted space for individuals who want to improve their health, strength, confidence, and overall lifestyle.

The gym welcomes both men and women and provides separate washrooms and showers to ensure privacy and comfort for all members. Global Fitness Botswana focuses on creating an inclusive environment where beginners, regular gym users, and professional lifters can all find a membership plan that suits their goals.

Membership Plans

Global Fitness Botswana offers three main membership plans. The Basic Plan is designed for beginners who need guidance and supervision from experienced mentors. The Gold Plan is ideal for regular members who want more flexibility and freedom to manage their own workout schedule. The Platinum Plan is made for professional lifters and includes all Gold Plan benefits, as well as access to the equipment loan service.

Facilities and Services

The company provides a wide range of fitness facilities and training spaces. These include swimming pools, basketball courts, boxing rings, and fully equipped gym sections for cardio, leg training, chest workouts, weights, and other fitness activities. This variety allows members to train in different ways and choose activities that match their interests and fitness goals.

Shop and Equipment

Global Fitness Botswana also includes a shop where customers can purchase fitness-related products. Items available include 15kg dumbbells, yoga mats, water bottles, and compression shirts. These products support members during training and help them continue their fitness routines outside the gym.

Contact Information

Customers can contact Global Fitness Botswana by email for more information about membership, facilities, products, and services. The company aims to make fitness more accessible by providing helpful communication and support to both new and existing members.
