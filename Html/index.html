WELCOME TO GLOBAL FITNESS

Established in 2010, Global Fitness Botswana has grown into a trusted and inclusive space dedicated to helping individuals achieve their health and wellness goals. We proudly serve both men and women, offering fully equipped facilities with separate washrooms and showers to ensure comfort and privacy for all.

This website is used to provide information about our services, membership plans, facilities, and available fitness products. All membership subscriptions and product purchases are completed directly through the company using our physical customer database. Customers must contact Global Fitness Botswana before subscribing to a membership plan or purchasing any shop items.

To subscribe, buy products, or ask questions about availability and pricing, please use the contact details provided on the Contact page.

Membership Plans

At Global Fitness Botswana, we provide a range of membership plans designed to suit different lifestyles:

  • Basic Plan (3 Months): Ideal for beginners, this plan allows access to the gym under the supervision of our experienced mentors.
  • Gold Plan (3 Months): Ideal for regulars, enjoy greater flexibility with full control over your workout schedule.
  • Platinum Plan (3 Months): Ideal for professional lifters, including all Gold benefits plus access to our equipment loan service.

Our facility offers a wide variety of services and training environments, including:

  • Swimming pools
  • Basketball courts
  • Boxing rings
  • Fully equipped gym sections for cardio, legs, pecs, weights, and more
